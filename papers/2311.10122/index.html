Video-LLaVA: Learning United Visual Representation by Alignment Before Projection

Lin, Bin、Ye, Yang、Zhu, Bin、Cui, Jiaxi、Ning, Munan、Jin, Peng、Yuan, Li

PKU-YuanGroup / 兔展 AIGC 联合实验室Video-LMM视频理解
原文 · AlphaXiv PDF代码

原始摘要

The Large Vision-Language Model (LVLM) has enhanced the performance of various downstream tasks in visual-language understanding. Most existing approaches encode images and videos into separate feature spaces, which are then fed as inputs to large language models. However, due to the lack of unified tokenization for images and videos, namely misalignment before projection, it becomes challenging for a Large Language Model (LLM) to learn multi-modal interactions from several poor projection layers. In this work, we unify visual representation into the language feature space to advance the foundational LLM towards a unified LVLM. As a result, we establish a simple but robust LVLM baseline, Video-LLaVA, which learns from a mixed dataset of images and videos, mutually enhancing each other. Video-LLaVA achieves superior performances on a broad range of 9 image benchmarks across 5 image question-answering datasets and 4 image benchmark toolkits. Additionally, our Video-LLaVA also outperforms Video-ChatGPT by 5.8%, 9.9%, 18.6%, and 10.1% on MSRVTT, MSVD, TGIF, and ActivityNet, respectively. Notably, extensive experiments demonstrate that Video-LLaVA mutually benefits images and videos within a unified visual representation, outperforming models designed specifically for images or videos. 论文概述

Video-LLaVA: Learning United Visual Representation by Alignment Before Projection：大视觉语言模型（LVLM）增强了视觉语言理解中各种下游任务的性能。大多数现有方法将图像和视频编码到单独的特征空间中，然后将其作为大型语言模型的输入。

核心贡献

Video-LLaVA: Learning United Visual Representation by Alignment Before Projection：在投影到语言模型之前先统一图像与视频的视觉表示，缓解两种模态分别编码造成的 token 空间错位，并建立统一图像/视频 LVLM 基线 Video-LLaVA。

方法描述

Video-LLaVA: Learning United Visual Representation by Alignment Before Projection：Video-LLaVA 先把图像和视频编码到统一视觉表示，再映射到语言特征空间供基础 LLM 使用；训练时混合图像与视频数据，使两类视觉输入在共享表示中相互促进。

数据集与资源

Video-LLaVA: Learning United Visual Representation by Alignment Before Projection：训练使用图像与视频混合数据，但摘要未给出数据集规模；官方代码入口已记录为 https://github.com/PKU-YuanGroup/Video-LLaVA。

评估与结果

Video-LLaVA: Learning United Visual Representation by Alignment Before Projection：模型覆盖 5 个图像问答数据集和 4 套图像评测工具，共 9 个图像基准；在 MSRVTT、MSVD、TGIF、ActivityNet 上分别比 Video-ChatGPT 高 5.8%、9.9%、18.6%、10.1%。
