Leveraging Vision-Centric Multi-Modal Expertise for 3D Object Detection

Linyan Huang、Zhiqi Li、Chonghao Sima、Wenhai Wang、Jingdong Wang、Yu Qiao、Hongyang Li

OpenDriveLab / HKU连续相机感知时序知识蒸馏三维目标检测
原文 · AlphaXiv PDF

原始摘要

Current research is primarily dedicated to advancing the accuracy of camera-only 3D object detectors through knowledge transferred from LiDAR- or multi-modal-based counterparts. However, the domain gap between LiDAR and camera features, coupled with incompatibility in temporal fusion, hinders distillation. We introduce VCD, including an apprentice-friendly multi-modal expert and temporal-fusion-friendly distillation supervision. VCD-E uses camera features and LiDAR depth priors. A fine-grained trajectory-based distillation module individually rectifies motion misalignment for each object. VCD-A sets a new state of the art on nuScenes with 63.1% NDS.

论文概述

用多模态教师改善纯相机三维检测，并专门解决时间融合时的特征域差距与物体运动错位。

核心贡献

VCD 同时设计相机友好的多模态教师 VCD-E 与适合时间融合的蒸馏监督，减少激光雷达教师和纯相机学生之间的特征域差距。

方法描述

VCD-E 保持与相机学生相同的网络结构，只把激光雷达作为深度先验；轨迹级细粒度蒸馏逐个修正运动物体在时间融合中的位置错位。

数据集与资源

摘要明确提到的数据集或评测环境包括 nuScenes。 摘要未公开代码。论文与团队链接保存在 source_urls。

评估与结果

VCD 在 nuScenes 三维检测基准上比较纯相机学生、多模态教师及不同蒸馏模块，核心指标为 NDS。 纯相机学生 VCD-A 在 nuScenes 达到 63.1% NDS，摘要称其刷新当时相机三维检测结果。
